\begin{center}
	\textbf{ABSTRACT}
\end{center}


When heavy ion nuclei collide with extremely high energies, in the orders of TeV, the nucleus matter gets compressed to such a high order that it disintegrates into its constituents. Furthermore, for a glimpse after the collision, a vastly interesting state of matter - the quark gluon plasma - is being produced. A hot state of matter in which quarks and gluons are not confined to baryons or mesons, but rather quasi-free. To investigate this state of matter, so called particle jets - highly energetic particles - are used to probe the QGP matter. By investigating the dispersive and dissipative effects of the QGP on these jets, elementary properties of the QGP can be deduced. In return, a direct effect of the particle jets on the QGP matter itself, so called diffusion wakes, have been proposed by theoretical calculations. This project aims  on measuring this phenomenon in existing ALICE collision data, taken in 2018 (RUN 2) during heavy ion (Pb-Pb) collisions and reviews a previous attempt of the CMS collaboration on this measurement.
