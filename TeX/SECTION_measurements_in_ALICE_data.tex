\section{Measurements in ALICE RUN 3 data}

\subsection{Data selection and preparation}

The data used for measuring the diffusion wake is Pb--Pb data from ALICE RUN 3 from the years 2023-2025. The raw data was filtered for general event requirements to make the diffusion wake analysis itself CPU time efficient and also to avoid using too much disk space. Events were required to have

\begin{itemize}
	\item a centrality of 0-10\%
	\item a primary vertex position $z \leq 10\bunit{cm}$
	\item at least one track with $|\eta| \leq 0.9$ and $p_T > 20\bunit{GeV}$ ($15\bunit{GeV}$ for 2025 data)
\end{itemize}

to be selected for the diffusion wake analysis. Details on the used datasets and the result of this stage of data selection can be seen in table \ref{table:RUN3_Data}.

\subsection{Single jet method measurements}

For the following chapters, it is important to give a quick notice on non-central collisions. When looking at two non-centrally colliding nuclei longitudinally, that is in beam direction, one should notice that the overlap region of the two discs has an initial approximately lenticular shape in the transverse plane, \cite{busza_heavy_2018} making the shape of the original QGP non-spherical. This results in anisotropies in the pressure of the hydrodynamic fluid, which in turn influences the expansion velocity and hence the azimuthal momentum distribution of the finally produced particles.

A simulation using the coupled linear Boltzmann transport and hydro (CoLBT-hydro) \cite{yang_3d_2023} has revealed the spatial properties of the expected diffusion wake.
